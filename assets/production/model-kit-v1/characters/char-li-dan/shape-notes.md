# 李旦

- 年代/候选：Chinese elder maritime merchant in Hirado about 1623, around sixty as a reconstruction candidate, stocky soft torso, round broad face, high forehead, thin arched brows, substantial greying pointed beard and moustache. Charcoal plain crossed-collar fine cloth robe, off-white undercollar, subdued brown sash, low soft dark scholar headcloth enclosing FULL unshaven hair. Flat cloth shoes. No official bird badge, no sword.
